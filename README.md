## 💻 About Me

I'm Arhaan Shaikh, an MCA student at Manipal University Jaipur with a B.Sc. in Computer Science from Mumbai University. I'm working toward becoming a Security Engineer, and I'm building the foundations the practical way: Linux, networking, hands-on labs, and written documentation of everything I learn.

> "I believe cybersecurity is about understanding systems before securing them."

That's the idea behind how I learn. I'd rather understand how computers and networks work, one command at a time, than take shortcuts. I'm currently interning in VAPT and SOC cybersecurity, and I keep a public record of my learning in my Cybersecurity Portfolio below.
